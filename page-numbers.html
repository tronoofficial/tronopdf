How to Add Page Numbers to PDF

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your document. The tool displays a live preview of the first page with page number placement.
2
Choose position and format — Click any of the 9 position buttons in the grid to place numbers. Select format (Page N, Page N of M, or custom), adjust font size, color and style.
3
Set options — Enter starting number, select page range (all pages or specific range), adjust margin, and enable "Skip first page" if you have a cover page.
4
Add and download — Click "Add Page Numbers" button. Your numbered PDF downloads instantly with all settings applied. All processing happens in your browser using pdf-lib - nothing is uploaded.
