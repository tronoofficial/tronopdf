PDF to Excel Online

Convert PDF tables and data to editable Excel (XLSX) spreadsheets instantly. Automatic row and column detection extracts tabular data. Choose one sheet per page or combine all pages into single spreadsheet. Live preview before conversion. Browser-based private processing.

How to Convert PDF to Excel

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your PDF with tables or data. The tool extracts text and detects table structure automatically.
2
Preview extracted data — See a live preview of the extracted table with detected rows and columns. Check if the data looks correct before converting.
3
Choose settings — Select sheet layout (one per page or combined), and adjust column gap sensitivity if columns aren't detected correctly. Normal works for most PDFs.
4
Convert and download — Click "Convert to Excel" button. Your PDF data becomes an editable .xlsx spreadsheet ready to download. All processing happens in your browser using pdf.js and SheetJS - nothing is uploaded.

🔒 Private Processing

TronoPDF processes all PDF to Excel conversions directly in your browser using pdf.js and SheetJS libraries. Your PDF files and extracted data are never uploaded to any server - they stay on your device. This means your financial statements, business reports, research data and sensitive spreadsheets remain private. Free to use with no signup required - convert as many PDFs to Excel as you need.
