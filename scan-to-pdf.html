How to Scan Documents to PDF

1
Open camera or upload — Click "Open Camera" to use your phone's camera (allow camera access when prompted), or click "Upload Photos" to select existing images from your device.
2
Capture pages — Point your camera at the document and tap the capture button. Add more pages by capturing additional documents or uploading more photos. Each capture becomes a PDF page.
3
Apply filters — Choose Color (original), Gray (grayscale for text documents), or B&W (high contrast for forms). Filters are applied to all pages automatically.
4
Download PDF — Click "Create PDF" button. Your scanned pages are combined into a clean multi-page PDF with no watermark. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded to any server.
