How to Convert Word to PDF

1
Upload your DOCX — Click "Select Word file" button or drag & drop your .docx document. The tool accepts any Word document created in Microsoft Word, Google Docs or other word processors.
2
Click Convert to PDF — Press the convert button. The tool renders your document page by page using Mammoth.js, preserving all formatting, images and layout.
3
Watch live progress — See real-time progress as each page is converted to PDF. Large documents show page-by-page conversion status so you know it's working.
4
Download PDF — Your Word document becomes a professional multi-page A4 PDF ready to download instantly. All processing happens in your browser using Mammoth.js and pdf-lib - nothing is uploaded.
