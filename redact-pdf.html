How to Redact PDF Files

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your document. The tool displays the first page ready for redaction.
2
Draw redaction rectangles — Click and drag rectangles over any text, names, numbers, SSN, addresses, or images you want to hide. Add as many rectangles as needed on each page.
3
Navigate pages — Use page controls to move between pages and add redactions where needed. You can undo individual rectangles or clear all if you make mistakes.
4
Apply redaction — Click "Apply Redaction" button. The tool converts redacted pages to images, then provides the secured PDF for download. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded.

🔒 Private Processing

TronoPDF processes all PDF redaction directly in your browser using pdf.js and HTML5 Canvas technology. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server - they stay on your device. This means your legal documents, medical records, financial statements, contracts with sensitive clauses, and other confidential information remain private. Unlike competitors that just add black boxes over text (which can be removed), our tool converts pages to images so redacted content cannot be copied or searched.
