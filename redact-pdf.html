How to Redact PDF Files

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your document. The tool displays the first page ready for redaction.
2
Draw redaction rectangles — Click and drag rectangles over any text, names, numbers, SSN, addresses, or images you want to hide. Add as many rectangles as needed on each page.
3
Navigate pages — Use page controls to move between pages and add redactions where needed. You can undo individual rectangles or clear all if you make mistakes.
4
Apply redaction — Click "Apply Redaction" button. The tool permanently removes redacted content by converting pages to images, then provides the secured PDF for download. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded.
