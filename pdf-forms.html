How to Create or Fill PDF Forms

1
Choose mode — Select "Create new form" to build from scratch, or "Fill existing form" to upload a PDF with form fields. The tool displays the first page for editing.
2
Add form fields — Click Text, Checkbox, Radio, Dropdown, Date or Signature buttons to add fields. For existing forms, the tool auto-detects fields and shows them ready to fill.
3
Position and configure — Drag fields to position them on the page. Set field names, default values, required status and other properties. Navigate to other pages to add more fields.
4
Download form — Click "Download PDF" button. Your fillable form (or filled form) downloads instantly with all fields functional. All processing happens in your browser using pdf-lib - nothing is uploaded.

🔒 100% Private & Secure

TronoPDF processes all PDF form operations directly in your browser using pdf-lib library. Your PDF files and form data are never uploaded to any server - they stay on your device. This means your government applications, legal contracts, business forms, surveys and sensitive documents remain completely private and secure.
