Why Convert PDF To Word?

Text-Based vs Scanned PDFs

📄 Text-Based PDFs

These are created from Word, Google Docs, or export tools. The text is selectable. Conversion works perfectly — you get clean, editable text.

📷 Scanned PDFs

These are photos of paper. The text is NOT selectable. For these, you need OCR (Optical Character Recognition). Use OCR PDF first, then convert to Word.

💡 Quick test: Try selecting text in your PDF. If you can highlight words, it's text-based. If not, it's scanned — use OCR first.

Tips For Best Results

  • Use text-based PDFs for perfect conversion
  • Run OCR on scans before converting
  • Check formatting after conversion — complex layouts may need small fixes
  • Keep the original PDF as a reference
  • Convert back to PDF after editing using Word to PDF

⚠️ Very complex layouts (multi-column magazines, heavy tables) may need manual adjustment in Word after conversion.
