Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TronoPDF.

🔍
💡 General
What is TronoPDF? ▾
TronoPDF is a free, browser-based toolkit with 38+ tools for PDFs and images — merge, split, compress, convert, sign, OCR, translate, and more. Everything runs inside your browser.
Is TronoPDF really free? ▾
Yes. Every tool is 100% free with no trials, watermarks, or hidden paywalls. Use everything, unlimited times.
Do I need to create an account? ▾
No. There's no signup, email, or password. Open any tool and start working immediately.
Which devices and browsers are supported? ▾
Any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) on desktop, tablet, or phone. You can also install it as an app.
🔒 Privacy & Security
Are my files uploaded to your servers? ▾
No. All processing happens on your device using your browser. Your files never leave your device — ever.
Is it safe to process confidential documents? ▾
Yes. Because nothing is uploaded, confidential legal, medical, or business documents never touch a server, making TronoPDF safer than upload-based tools.
Do you store my files after I close the page? ▾
No. Files exist only in your browser's memory and are discarded when you close or refresh the page.
What data do you collect? ▾
Only anonymous, aggregate analytics (page views). We do not collect your files, name, or email. See our Privacy Policy.
🛠️ Tools & Usage
How do I compress a PDF to a specific size? ▾
Open Compress PDF, enable "Set target file size", enter your target (e.g., 100 KB), and compress. The tool auto-adjusts quality to hit your goal.
Can I process multiple files at once? ▾
Yes. Tools like Compress PDF support batch processing — select multiple files and get a result card for each.
What is the maximum file size? ▾
There's no server limit since files stay on your device. Very large files depend on your device's available memory.
Why is my converted file bigger or smaller than expected? ▾
Output size depends on the source content (images, pages, fonts). Try a different compression level to balance quality and size.
Can I use TronoPDF offline? ▾
Once loaded (or installed as an app), many tools work offline. An internet connection is needed the first time to load the libraries.
💰 Pricing & Limits
Are there any usage limits? ▾
No daily or monthly limits. Use every tool as much as you want.
Is there a premium version? ▾
No. There is no premium tier — everything is free for everyone.
Can I use it for commercial work? ▾
Yes. Personal and commercial use are both allowed, free of charge.
⚙️ Technical
What technologies power TronoPDF? ▾
Web Workers, pdf-lib, pdf.js, and modern browser APIs — all running client-side for speed and privacy.
Why does a tool show an error? ▾
Usually a corrupted or password-protected file. Try unlocking it first, or refresh and retry. Report persistent bugs via Contact.
How do I install TronoPDF as an app? ▾
Click the "Open in app" / Install option in your browser, or use the footer's Install App button. It then works like a native app.
