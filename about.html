About TronoPDF

The free, privacy-first PDF toolkit that runs entirely in your browser.

👑 Our Mission

TronoPDF was built with one simple belief: working with PDFs should be free, fast, and private for everyone. You shouldn't need an account, a subscription, or to hand your confidential documents to a stranger's server just to merge, compress, or convert a file.

That's why every single tool on TronoPDF runs 100% inside your browser. Your files never leave your device — ever.

📖 Our Story

Like many ideas, TronoPDF started with frustration. Needing to compress a PDF for an online form, the only "free" options uploaded the file to unknown servers, added watermarks, or demanded a subscription at the last step.

We asked: "With modern browsers, why can't the browser itself do the work?" The answer was — it can. Using technologies like Web Workers and open-source libraries (pdf-lib, pdf.js), we built a toolkit where your own computer does the processing.

Today TronoPDF offers 38+ tools for PDFs and images, and we're adding more all the time.

💜 Our Values

  • Privacy is non-negotiable. If a feature can't be built without uploading your files, we won't build it that way.
  • Free means free. We keep the lights on without charging you or selling your data.
  • Simplicity wins. One click to start, no confusing steps, no dark patterns.
  • Quality over quantity. We'd rather have fewer tools that work perfectly than many that half-work.
