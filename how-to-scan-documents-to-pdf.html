Tips For Perfect Scans

  • Good lighting — Natural light or bright room, avoid shadows
  • Flat surface — Place document on a dark, contrasting background
  • Hold steady — Keep camera parallel to the page to avoid skew
  • Fill the frame — Get close so text is sharp and readable
  • One page per photo — Don't cram multiple pages in one shot
  • Use B&W filter — For text documents, black & white gives crisp results

💡 Pro tip: After scanning, run OCR PDF to make the text searchable and selectable.

Choosing The Right Filter

  • Color — For photos, certificates with stamps, colored documents
  • Grayscale — For documents with some color, smaller file size
  • Black & White — For pure text documents, smallest size, crispest text
