🐞 Report A Bug

To help us fix issues faster, include:

  • The tool name (e.g., Compress PDF)
  • Your browser & device (e.g., Chrome on Android)
  • What you expected vs. what happened
  • A screenshot if possible

💡 Request A Feature

TronoPDF is shaped by its users. If there's a tool or feature you wish existed, tell us! Popular requests are prioritized on our roadmap.

❓ Quick Answers

  • Is TronoPDF free? Yes — free to use, no signup required.
  • Are my files uploaded? For supported tools, everything runs in your browser and files stay on your device. PDF translation sends extracted text to Google Translate (see Privacy Policy).
  • Do I need an account? No signup required.
  • Where is my data stored? For supported tools, data stays on your device and is not stored by us.
