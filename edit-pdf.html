How to Edit PDF Documents

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your document. The tool displays the first page for editing.
2
Choose editing tool — Select Text (with fonts and formatting), Draw (freehand pen), Image (upload photos/logos), or Shapes (rectangles, ellipses, lines) from the toolbar.
3
Customize and position — Click Add to place the element, then drag it anywhere on the page. Resize using corner handles, adjust opacity, reorder layers, and zoom for precision.
4
Apply and download — Click "Apply & Download" button. Your edited PDF will be ready instantly with all changes saved. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded.
