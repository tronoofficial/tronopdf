Why Add Page Numbers To A PDF?

Page numbers make documents professional and easy to navigate. Common reasons include:

  • Academic submissions — theses, assignments, research papers
  • Business reports — annual reports, proposals, audits
  • Legal documents — contracts, court filings, agreements
  • Books and manuals — multi-page publications
  • Meeting documents — agendas and minutes for reference
  • Printed materials — easier to organize physical copies

Position And Format Options

📍 Positions

  • Bottom-right — Most common, professional default
  • Bottom-center — Classic book style
  • Bottom-left — Alternative layout
  • Top-right / Top-center / Top-left — Header style

🔢 Formats

  • Simple — 1, 2, 3, 4...
  • With label — Page 1, Page 2...
  • With total — Page 1 of 10

💡 Pro tip: Bottom-right with "Page X of Y" is the most professional choice for business and academic documents.

Tips For Best Results

  • Skip cover pages — Start numbering from page 2 if needed
  • Use subtle styling — Small font, gray color looks professional
  • Match margins — Keep numbers within page margins
  • Check existing numbers — Don't double-number if already present
  • Preview before download — Verify position on a few pages

⚠️ If your PDF already has page numbers, adding new ones will create duplicates. Remove old numbers first or choose a different position.
