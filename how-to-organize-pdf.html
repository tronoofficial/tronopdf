Why Organize A PDF?

  • Fix page order — Scanned pages often come out shuffled

Tips For Best Results

  • Preview thumbnails — Check each page before finalizing
  • Keep the original — Organizing creates a new file
  • Number your plan — Note desired order before rearranging
  • Combine with merge — Use Merge PDF for whole files
  • Compress after — Use Compress PDF if needed

⚠️ Deleting pages is permanent in the output file. Always keep your original PDF as backup.

Organize vs Split vs Merge — Which To Use?

  • Organize — Reorder/delete/insert pages within one document
  • Split — Extract pages into separate new files
  • Merge — Combine multiple complete PDFs into one

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I insert pages from another PDF?

Yes. Add pages from a second PDF at any position in your document.

Does organizing reduce quality?

No. Pages are kept as-is. Only the order changes.

Can I undo a deletion?

During editing, yes. After downloading, no — keep your original as backup.

Is there a page limit?

No server limit since processing happens in your browser. Very large files depend on your device's memory.
