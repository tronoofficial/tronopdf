What Gets Preserved

  • All slides — Each slide becomes a PDF page
  • Text and fonts — All text content
  • Images and graphics — Photos, charts, diagrams
  • Basic formatting — Colors, bold, italic
  • Slide layout — Title and content placement

⚠️ Animations, transitions, and embedded videos do not appear in the PDF. They are presentation-only features.

Tips For Best Results

  • Use standard fonts — Arial, Calibri for best compatibility
  • Embed images — Ensure images are inserted, not linked
  • Check slide size — Widescreen (16:9) or Standard (4:3)
  • Review before sharing — Open the PDF to verify
  • Keep original PPTX — For future edits and animations
  • Compress if needed — Use Compress PDF for smaller file

Frequently Asked Questions

Will animations work in the PDF?

No. PDFs are static. Animations are presentation-only features.

Does it work with PPT and PPTX?

Yes. Both older PPT and newer PPTX formats are supported.

Will videos in slides appear?

Videos are not embedded in PDF. They'll appear as static images or may not show.

Is there a file size limit?

No server limit since processing happens in your browser. Very large files depend on your device's memory.
