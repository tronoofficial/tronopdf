Page Size And Orientation Settings

Choosing the right settings makes your PDF look professional:

  • A4 (210 × 297 mm) — Standard for most countries, perfect for documents and forms
  • Letter (8.5 × 11 inches) — Standard in the US and Canada
  • Fit to page — Images automatically resize to fill the page
  • Original size — Images keep their original dimensions (may leave white space)

Portrait vs Landscape

  • Portrait — Tall pages, best for documents and text-heavy content
  • Landscape — Wide pages, best for photos and horizontal images

💡 Pro tip: For government forms and applications, always use A4 portrait unless the form specifically asks for a different size.

Tips For Best Results

  • Use clear, high-resolution images — blurry photos will look worse in PDF
  • Crop images first — use Image Crop to remove unnecessary borders
  • Compress large images — use Image Compressor if your PDF becomes too large
  • Arrange images in order — drag to reorder before converting
  • Check margins — add white space if images touch the page edges
  • Preview before downloading — make sure everything looks correct

⚠️ For exam or government applications, always check the specific requirements for image size, format, and file size limits before uploading.
