How PDF Translation Works

Step 1: Text Extraction (In Your Browser)

When you upload a PDF, the tool extracts all text content directly in your browser using PDF.js. Your original PDF file never leaves your device during this step.

Step 2: Translation (Google Translate)

The extracted text is sent securely via HTTPS to Google Translate, which provides the translation. This is the only step where data leaves your device.

Step 3: PDF Reconstruction (In Your Browser)

The translated text is combined with your original PDF structure to create a new PDF file. This step happens entirely in your browser.

Step 4: Download

Your translated PDF is generated in your browser and downloaded directly to your device.

Best Practices for PDF Translation

✅ Before Translating

  • Check sensitivity — Consider if the document contains confidential information
  • Verify text-based PDF — Scanned/image PDFs need OCR first (use OCR PDF)
  • Know the context — Technical, legal, and medical translations may need professional review
  • Keep original — Always keep a copy of the original untranslated document

✅ After Translating

  • Review the translation — Machine translation can make mistakes, especially with idioms and technical terms
  • Check formatting — Some languages may have different text lengths affecting layout
  • Verify key terms — Technical and legal terms may need manual verification
  • Professional review — For important documents, have a human translator review machine output
💡 Pro tip: For best results, scan the translated output carefully for: numbers, dates, proper nouns, technical terms, and any content where translation errors could cause problems.

Limitations of Machine Translation

What Machine Translation Does Well

  • Speed — Translate hundreds of pages in seconds
  • Convenience — Instant access to 50+ languages
  • Cost — Free for most use cases
  • General understanding — Get the gist of foreign documents

Where Machine Translation Falls Short

  • Context — May miss cultural and contextual nuances
  • Precision — Legal, medical, technical terms may be inaccurate
  • Style — Formal/informal tone may not match original
  • Idioms — Figurative language may translate literally
  • Ambiguity — Words with multiple meanings may be wrong
💡 Best practice: Use machine translation for general understanding and initial review. For critical documents, use machine translation as a first pass, then have qualified human translators review and refine the output.

Language-Specific Considerations

🇮🇳 Indic Languages (Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, etc.)

  • Complex scripts may have font rendering issues
  • Compound words may split unexpectedly
  • Honorifics and formal language may simplify

🇨🇳 Chinese, Japanese, Korean

  • Character-based writing may lose some nuance
  • Idioms and proverbs may translate literally
  • Honorifics and politeness levels may not transfer

🇸🇦 Arabic, Hebrew, Persian

  • Right-to-left text direction preserved
  • Religious and cultural terms may need verification
  • Dialect variations may not be distinguished

🇷🇺 Russian, Ukrainian, Eastern European

  • Cyrillic scripts translate well
  • Grammatical cases may simplify
  • Cultural references may need context

Troubleshooting Common Issues

❌ Text Not Extracted

  • Scanned PDF: Run OCR PDF first to extract text
  • Image-based PDF: Use PDF to JPG to view, then OCR
  • Protected PDF: Use Unlock PDF first

❌ Poor Translation Quality

  • Technical content: May need human translator for precision
  • Idioms: Translate phrase-by-phrase for better results
  • Formatting issues: Simplify complex layouts before translating

❌ Missing Content

  • Images with text: OCR images separately, then translate
  • Headers/footers: May not be extracted consistently
  • Tables: Complex tables may lose structure

Alternative Approaches

🔄 For Better Accuracy

  • Translate then edit: Use machine translation, then manually refine
  • Hybrid approach: Translate main text, manually translate key terms

📋 For Different Output Formats

  • PDF to Markdown → translate → format: Developer-friendly workflow
