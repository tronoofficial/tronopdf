Why Do Forms Require Such Small Sizes?

Government and exam portals limit upload sizes to keep their servers fast. Typical requirements in India:

  • Photo — 20 KB to 50 KB (JPG)
  • Signature — 10 KB to 20 KB (JPG)
  • Documents — 50 KB to 500 KB depending on portal

Examples of portals with strict limits: SSC, UPSC, IBPS Banking, Railways (RRB), NEET, JEE, state police and court recruitment sites. If your file is even 1 KB over the limit, the form rejects it.

Typical Portal Requirements (India)

Keep this quick reference handy while filling forms:

  • SSC — Photo: 20-50 KB, Signature: 10-20 KB
  • UPSC — Photo: 20-300 KB, Signature: 10-120 KB (check notice)
  • IBPS / SBI — Photo: 20-50 KB, Signature: 10-20 KB
  • RRB — Photo: 20-50 KB, Signature: 10-40 KB
  • NEET / JEE — Photo: 10-200 KB, Signature: 3-20 KB (check notice)

💡 Pro tip: Also check the required dimensions (e.g., 3.5 × 4.5 cm photo). Use Image Resizer first for exact pixels, then compress to the KB limit.

How To Keep Quality While Shrinking Size

  • Start with a good photo — Sharp input survives compression better
  • Resize first — A 200x250 px photo compresses to 20KB with far better quality than a 4000 px photo
  • Use JPG — Best format for photos at small sizes
  • Plain background — Simple backgrounds compress cleaner
  • Avoid re-compressing — Always compress from the original, not an already-compressed copy

⚠️ If your face becomes unrecognizable at 20KB, reduce the pixel dimensions first, then compress. A smaller sharp photo beats a large blurry one.

Common Mistakes To Avoid

  • ❌ Renaming instead of compressing — Changing the file name does NOT change size
  • ❌ Screenshotting the photo — Screenshots add noise and wrong dimensions
  • ❌ Using PNG for photos — PNG stays large; convert to JPG
  • ❌ Ignoring dimensions — Portals check pixels too, not just KB
  • ❌ Uploading without verifying — Right-click → Properties to confirm size before submitting
