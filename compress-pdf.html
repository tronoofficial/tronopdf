How to Compress PDF Effectively

Compress PDF to 100KB for Government Forms

Many government portals in India (SSC, UPSC, banking exams, railways) require PDF uploads under strict size limits like 100KB or 200KB. Here's how to hit those exact targets:

  • Use Target Size mode — Enter 100 in the KB field and let the tool automatically adjust compression
  • Start with text-based PDFs — Text compresses much better than scanned images
  • For scanned PDFs — Run OCR first with our OCR PDF tool to extract text, then compress
  • Check file properties — Right-click → Properties to verify exact size before uploading
💡 Pro tip: If your PDF is still over 100KB after compression, try splitting it into smaller parts with our Split PDF tool, compress each part separately, then recombine only what you need.

Best Settings for Scanned Documents

Scanned PDFs are often 5-10x larger than text-based PDFs because every page is essentially a high-resolution image. Here's how to compress them effectively:

  • Use Extreme compression — Scanned documents benefit most from aggressive compression
  • Run OCR first — Convert image text to actual text with OCR PDF, then compress for 70-90% size reduction
  • Crop margins — Remove scanner edges with Crop PDF before compressing
  • Rotate if needed — Fix sideways pages with Rotate PDF for cleaner compression

Compress PDF for Email Attachments

Most email services (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo) have 25MB attachment limits. Here's how to compress large PDFs for email:

  • Files under 50MB — Use Recommended compression for good balance of size and quality
  • Files 50-100MB — Use Extreme compression to get under 25MB
  • Files over 100MB — Split into multiple parts first, compress each, send separately
  • Check before sending — Verify compressed size matches your email provider's limit
💡 Pro tip: For very large PDFs, consider using cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox) and sharing a link instead of attaching the file directly.

Reduce PDF Without Losing Readability

Text-based PDFs (created from Word, Excel, or other applications) can be compressed significantly without quality loss:

  • Images are the main size culprits — Our smart compression reduces image quality only where it won't affect readability
  • Text stays crisp — Vector text in PDFs is never compressed, so fonts remain sharp
  • Recommended level works best — For most text documents, 40-60% size reduction with no visible quality loss
  • Test before sharing — Download and open the compressed file to verify everything looks good

Common Compression Scenarios

📋 Job Applications & Exam Forms

Most require PDFs under 100-200KB. Use Target Size mode with exact KB value. Combine multiple documents (resume, certificates, ID) into one PDF with Merge PDF, then compress to meet size limits.

📊 Business Reports & Proposals

Use Recommended compression for documents with charts and images. Preserves professional appearance while reducing email attachment size. Add password protection after compression for confidential reports.

📚 Academic Papers & Theses

Large research papers (50-100+ pages) benefit from Less compression to maintain chart and graph quality. Use Extreme only for text-heavy documents without complex graphics.

🏛️ Legal Documents & Contracts

Use Recommended or Less compression to preserve document integrity. After compression, add digital signatures and password protection for security.

⚠️ Important: Always keep your original uncompressed PDF as backup. Compression is permanent in the output file, and you may need the full-quality version later.

Understanding Compression Levels

🔥 Extreme Compression

Best for: Scanned documents, large files needing maximum size reduction, text-heavy PDFs where some quality loss is acceptable.

Size reduction: 70-90% smaller

Quality impact: Images may appear slightly blurry, but text remains readable

⚖️ Recommended Compression

Best for: Most documents, business reports, academic papers, general use

Size reduction: 40-60% smaller

Quality impact: Minimal - good balance of size and quality

✨ Less Compression

Best for: High-quality prints, documents with detailed graphics, legal contracts, presentations

Size reduction: 20-40% smaller

Quality impact: Almost none - maintains original quality
