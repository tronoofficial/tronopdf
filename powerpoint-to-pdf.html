How to Convert PowerPoint to PDF

1
Select your presentation — Click "Select PowerPoint" button or drag & drop your .pptx/.ppt file. You can also click "Slide Images" to upload JPG/PNG slides, or "Try sample slides" to test without a file.
2
Preview your slides — See live preview of all rendered slides in the tool. The tool shows slide count and renders each slide exactly as it appears in PowerPoint.
3
Click Convert to PDF — Press the convert button. The tool captures each slide as a high-quality image and builds a multi-page PDF with correct aspect ratio.
4
Download PDF — Your presentation becomes a professional PDF with every slide as a separate page. All processing happens in your browser using PPTXjs, html2canvas and jsPDF - nothing is uploaded.
