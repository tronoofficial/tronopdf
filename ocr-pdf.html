How to OCR PDF Files

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your scanned or digital PDF. The tool supports any PDF with text content, including government forms and scanned documents.
2
Choose language — Leave on Auto-detect (recommended) to let the tool identify the script automatically, or manually select from supported languages including Hindi, Arabic, Chinese and more.
3
Click Extract Text — Press the extract button. The tool reads every page, detects broken text automatically, and uses OCR (Tesseract.js) to recover correct characters where needed.
4
Copy or download — Review the extracted text in the live preview, then copy to clipboard or download as a TXT file. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded to any server.

🔒 Private Processing

TronoPDF processes all OCR operations directly in your browser using Tesseract.js, a widely-used open-source OCR engine. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server - they stay on your device. This means your sensitive documents, government forms, legal papers, medical records and business files remain private.
