How to Convert PDF to Markdown

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your document. The tool accepts any PDF with text content, including research papers, notes and documentation.
2
Choose conversion options — Enable heading detection, list detection, and page separators as needed. The tool analyzes font sizes to identify document structure automatically.
3
Click Convert to Markdown — Press the convert button. The tool extracts text from every page, detects headings and lists, and produces clean Markdown output in the preview.
4
Copy or download — Review the Markdown preview, then click "Copy" to copy to clipboard or "Download .md" to save as a file. All processing happens in your browser using pdf.js - nothing is uploaded.
