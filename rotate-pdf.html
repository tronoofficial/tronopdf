How to Rotate PDF Pages

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your document. The tool displays thumbnails of all pages showing their current orientation.
2
Choose rotation method — Click individual page thumbnails to rotate specific pages, or use bulk rotate buttons to rotate all pages at once. Each click rotates 90° clockwise.
3
Preview orientation — Watch the thumbnails update in real-time as you rotate pages. Verify that every page is now in the correct orientation before applying.
4
Apply and download — Click "Apply Rotation" button. Your PDF downloads instantly with all pages rotated to their new orientation. All processing happens in your browser using pdf-lib - nothing is uploaded to any server.
