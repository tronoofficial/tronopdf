Why Convert PDF To JPG?

Converting PDF pages to images is useful in many situations:

  • Presentations — insert PDF pages as images into PowerPoint or Google Slides

  • Design work — use PDF pages as reference images in design software
  • Print previews — view how a PDF will look before printing
  • Archiving — save PDF pages as images for long-term storage

JPG is the most universally supported image format — it works everywhere, on every device and platform.
