Two Types Of PDF Forms

✅ Fillable (Interactive) Forms

These have built-in fields — text boxes, checkboxes, dropdowns. Click a field and type. Most government and business forms are fillable.

❌ Non-Fillable (Flat) Forms

These are just pages with printed lines. Many people print and handwrite these — but you can fill them digitally by adding text on top using Edit PDF or the forms tool's text mode.

💡 Quick test: Open the PDF and click where you should type. If a cursor appears, it's fillable. If nothing happens, it's flat.

Tips For Perfect Form Filling

  • Read instructions first — Some forms require specific formats (DD/MM/YYYY)
  • Use Tab key — Move quickly between fields
  • Double-check numbers — IDs, dates, and amounts cause most rejections
  • Don't leave required fields blank — Use "N/A" where allowed
  • Save a copy — Keep the filled PDF for your records
  • Sign last — Fill everything, then add the signature
  • Flatten if asked — Some portals want a non-editable final file

⚠️ Filled forms contain sensitive personal data (ID numbers, addresses). Never fill them on websites that upload your file — use browser-based tools like TronoPDF.
