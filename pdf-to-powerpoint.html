How to Convert PDF to PowerPoint

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your document. The tool displays thumbnail previews of all pages for review.
2
Preview your slides — Review the page thumbnails to ensure correct content and order. The tool shows up to 24 pages in preview (all pages will be converted).
3
Choose quality level — Select High (2x - recommended for presentations), Medium (1.5x - balanced), or Standard (1x - smaller file size) based on your needs.
4
Convert and download — Click "Convert to PowerPoint" button. Your PDF becomes a .pptx file with every page as a slide. All processing happens in your browser using pdf.js and PptxGenJS - nothing is uploaded.
