Choosing the Right Page Size for Your Country

Different countries use different standard paper sizes. Choose based on your destination or purpose:

📐 A4 (210 × 297 mm) - World Standard

Used in: Europe, Asia, Africa, South America, Australia, and most countries worldwide

Best for: Visa applications, academic papers, business documents, official forms

Tip: When in doubt, use A4 - it's the most universally accepted size.

📏 Letter (8.5 × 11 inches) - North American Standard

Used in: United States, Canada, Mexico, and some Central American countries

Best for: US visa applications, American university submissions, US business documents

⚖️ Legal (8.5 × 14 inches) - US Legal Documents

Used in: United States for specific legal documents

Best for: Contracts, deeds, legal filings in the US

🖼️ Match Image Size - Photography & Portfolios

Best for: Photo albums, art portfolios, photography presentations where you want to preserve original image dimensions without adding margins or fitting to a standard page.

Portrait vs Landscape: When to Use Each

📄 Portrait (Vertical) - Use For:

  • Official documents (passports, IDs, certificates)
  • Letters and contracts
  • Receipts and invoices
  • Standard forms and applications
  • Portrait-oriented photographs

🏞️ Landscape (Horizontal) - Use For:

  • Wide photographs (group photos, landscapes)
  • Certificates and diplomas (many are horizontal)
  • Presentation slides and charts
  • Wide documents (newspaper clippings, maps)
  • Business cards and ID cards

Best Practices for Different Document Types

📋 Scanned Documents Workflow

For scanning paper documents to PDF:

  • Scan at 300 DPI for text documents (balance of quality and size)
  • Use 600 DPI for documents with fine details or small text
  • Scan in color for documents with stamps, signatures, or colored elements
  • Scan in grayscale for black-and-white text documents (smaller file size)
  • After conversion, run OCR PDF to make text searchable

🖼️ Photo Albums Workflow

For creating photo albums or portfolios:

  • Use "Match Image Size" to preserve original photo dimensions
  • Set margins to zero for full-bleed photo prints
  • Group by theme — arrange related photos together
  • After conversion, compress with Compress PDF for email sharing

🛂 Visa Application Workflow

Complete workflow for visa document preparation:

  • Step 1: Scan each document individually at 300 DPI
  • Step 2: Crop to remove scanner edges with Crop PDF if needed
  • Step 3: Convert to PDF using A4 (or Letter for US) page size
  • Step 4: Use portrait orientation for most documents
  • Step 5: Merge related documents with Merge PDF
  • Step 6: Compress to meet embassy file size limits
  • Step 7: Add password protection with Protect PDF for sensitive documents

🧾 Receipts & Expense Reports

For business expense documentation:

  • Photograph receipts with good lighting and flat surface
  • Crop tightly using Image Crop to remove background
  • Merge all receipts for one expense period into one PDF
  • Use Letter size (for US companies) or A4 (worldwide)

Tips for Best Conversion Results

✅ Image Quality Best Practices

  • High resolution source — Use at least 150 DPI, ideally 300 DPI
  • Good lighting — Avoid shadows and glare when photographing documents
  • Flat surface — Place documents on a flat, contrasting background
  • Straight alignment — Keep the camera parallel to the document
  • Sharp focus — Ensure text and details are crisp before converting

✅ Margins and Layout

  • Use margins for documents — 10-20mm margins look professional
  • Zero margins for photos — Set margins to 0 for full-page prints
  • Consistent orientation — Keep all images in the same orientation for uniform PDFs
  • Logical order — Arrange images in reading sequence before converting

✅ File Organization

  • Name files clearly — Use descriptive names before uploading (e.g., "passport-front.jpg")
  • Group related images — Convert related images together for easier management
  • Keep originals — Always preserve the source images as backup
  • Document the order — Note the sequence if converting complex multi-page documents

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Don't Do This:

  • Low-resolution images — Blurry images become unreadable in PDF
  • Wrong page size for destination — Using Letter size for European visa applications
  • Mixed orientations without purpose — Randomly mixing portrait and landscape confuses readers
  • Uploading without previewing — Always check the converted PDF before submitting
  • Over-compressing — Excessive compression makes text unreadable
  • Ignoring color profile — Photos may look different when printed vs on screen
⚠️ Important: For official submissions (visas, court documents, academic papers), always verify the specific requirements of the receiving authority. Some embassies and institutions have strict specifications for page size, resolution, and file format.
