Privacy Policy

The short version: for supported tools, your files are processed locally in your browser and we collect as little as technically possible. Only PDF translation (and the optional interface translator) sends extracted text to Google Translate - your original PDF file stays on your device.

🔒 Privacy by design: For supported tools, TronoPDF processes all files locally in your browser. We do not upload, store, or see your documents. The only exception is PDF translation, which sends extracted text to Google Translate (see Section 6).

1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TronoPDF ("we", "us") handles information when you use our website and tools. We built TronoPDF to be privacy-first, so this policy is short and honest: we collect almost nothing, and for supported tools your files are never transmitted to us.

2 Your Files & Documents

For supported tools, all PDF and image processing (merge, split, compress, convert, edit, sign, protect, watermark, and many more) happens entirely on your device using your browser's own computing power. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, never stored by us, and never visible to us. When you close or refresh the page, any in-memory data is discarded.

3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your files or their contents.
  • We do not require accounts, names, or emails.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other websites.

4 Information We May Collect

We use privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand basic usage, such as page views and general location (country-level). This data is anonymous and statistical — it does not identify you and does not include your files.

We also store small preferences (like your dark-mode choice or tool ratings) in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and can be cleared anytime by clearing browser data.

5 Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We may use local storage to remember preferences (theme, ratings). You can remove this at any time via your browser settings.

6 Third-Party Services

To work, the site loads open-source libraries (e.g., pdf-lib, pdf.js) from public CDNs, and is delivered via Cloudflare. These providers may receive standard technical data (like your IP address) as part of serving the files, but they do not receive your documents.

Translation services: The PDF translation tool sends the extracted text of your document to Google Translate over HTTPS to produce the translation. The optional interface language selector also uses Google Translate. These services receive only the text needed for translation - not your original PDF file, images, or layout. Google Translate processes this text under Google's privacy policy. For documents requiring absolute privacy (legal, medical, confidential), consider using our other tools that process files 100% locally without any external API calls.

7 Data Security

For supported tools, because your files never leave your device, the primary risk of "upload-based" tools simply doesn't exist here. The site is served over HTTPS to protect the integrity of the code you receive.

8 Children's Privacy

Our service is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we collect essentially no personal data, there is nothing to protect more than standard browser privacy controls already provide.

9 Your Rights & Choices

You can clear local storage and analytics-related data via your browser settings at any time. Since we hold no personal accounts or files, there is no personal data for us to export or delete on our side.

10 Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
