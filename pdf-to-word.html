How to Convert PDF to Word

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop any PDF file. The tool supports any PDF with text or scanned pages.
2
Choose settings — Select content mode (Text + Image, Text only, or Image only) and page range. Language is detected automatically from the document's script.
3
Click Convert to Word — The tool extracts text from every page, fixes broken characters with OCR if needed, and builds a preview showing every page's content.
4
Preview & download — Review the live preview of every page, then download your editable .doc file. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded.

🔒 100% Private & Secure

TronoPDF processes all conversions directly in your browser using open-source libraries (PDF.js, Tesseract.js). Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server - they stay on your device. This means your documents remain completely private and secure, even for sensitive legal, medical, government or business files in any language.
