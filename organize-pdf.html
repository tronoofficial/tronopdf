How to Organize PDF Pages

1
Upload your PDF(s) — Click "Select PDF files" button or drag & drop one or more PDF files. All pages appear as thumbnails in a grid view.
2
Reorder, delete or rotate — Drag page thumbnails to reorder them, click ✕ to delete unwanted pages, or use the rotate button to fix orientation. Add more PDFs to combine pages from different files.
3
Preview your changes — Review the visual grid to ensure pages are in the correct order. Use "Reset all" to start over if needed.
4
Click Organize — Press the "Organize PDF" button. Your reorganized PDF downloads instantly with all changes applied. All processing happens in your browser using pdf-lib - nothing is uploaded.

🔒 100% Private & Secure

TronoPDF processes all PDF organization directly in your browser using pdf-lib library. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server - they stay on your device. This means your documents remain completely private and secure, even for sensitive legal, medical, financial or business files. No file size limits, no daily limits, no watermarks, no signup required - organize as many PDFs as you need.
