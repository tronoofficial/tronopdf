How to Crop Images Online

1
Upload your photo — Click "Select Image" button or drag & drop any JPG/PNG file. The tool displays your image with a crop overlay.
2
Select crop area — Drag the crop box handles to select the area you want to keep, or click an aspect ratio preset (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, passport) to lock the proportions automatically.
3
Rotate if needed — Use the Rotate button to turn your image 90 degrees at a time. Adjust the crop box position after rotation to get the perfect framing.
4
Crop and download — Click "Crop Image" button. Choose JPG or PNG format, adjust quality if needed, and download your cropped image instantly. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded.
