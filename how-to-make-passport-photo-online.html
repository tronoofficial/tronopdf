Passport Photo Sizes By Country

Different countries require different dimensions. TronoPDF auto-sets the correct size when you pick a country:

  • India (Passport/PAN/OCI) — 35 × 45 mm (3.5 × 4.5 cm)
  • India (some exam forms) — 3.5 × 4.5 cm or 2 × 2 inch as specified
  • United States (Passport/Visa) — 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm)
  • UK / EU / Schengen Visa — 35 × 45 mm
  • Canada — 50 × 70 mm
  • Australia — 35 × 45 mm
  • China (Visa) — 33 × 48 mm

💡 Pro tip: Always check the exact specification on the official form you are filling. Some Indian exam portals (SSC, UPSC, NEET) specify their own pixel and KB limits.

Official Photo Requirements Checklist

Most passport and visa authorities follow these rules. Check each one before submitting:

  • ✅ Plain white or off-white background
  • ✅ Neutral expression — both eyes open, mouth closed
  • ✅ Face the camera directly — no tilt or rotation
  • ✅ Even lighting — no shadows on face or background
  • ✅ No glasses (most countries now require this)
  • ✅ No hats or headgear (except religious, face fully visible)
  • ✅ Recent photo — taken within the last 6 months
  • ✅ Face covers 70-80% of the photo height
  • ✅ High resolution — sharp, not pixelated or blurred

How To Take The Perfect Photo At Home

💡 Lighting

Stand facing a window in daylight. Avoid direct sun (harsh shadows) and overhead-only light (eye shadows).

🧱 Background

Stand 1 meter in front of a plain white or light wall. A white bedsheet pinned to the wall also works.

📱 Camera

Use the rear camera (better quality) at eye level, about 1-1.5 meters away. Ask someone to take it, or use a timer.

🧍 Posture

Stand straight, shoulders level, remove glasses, keep hair off the face, look directly at the lens.

💡 Pro tip: Take 5-10 photos and pick the sharpest one with the most even lighting. You have unlimited free tries!

Top 7 Reasons Passport Photos Get Rejected

  • ❌ Shadows on the face or background
  • ❌ Low resolution or blurry image
  • ❌ Wrong size or face too small/large in frame
  • ❌ Colored or patterned background
  • ❌ Smiling with teeth showing
  • ❌ Glasses causing glare
  • ❌ Old photo (appearance changed)

⚠️ A rejected photo can delay your passport or visa by weeks. Follow the checklist above and use the correct dimensions to avoid this.

Passport Photo vs Online Form Photo

  • Physical passport/visa — Needs printed photos on matte/glossy paper
  • Online forms (exams, jobs) — Needs a digital JPG with size limits (often 20-50 KB)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the passport photo size in India?

Standard is 35 × 45 mm (3.5 × 4.5 cm). Some forms specify 2 × 2 inch — always check the form's instructions.

Can I use a selfie?

Not recommended. Selfies distort facial proportions and often have shadows. Ask someone to take the photo or use a timer.

Can I wear glasses?

Most countries now require no glasses. Remove them to avoid rejection.

Does the tool change my background to white?
