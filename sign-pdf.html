How to Sign PDF Documents

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your document. The tool supports any PDF file you need to sign.
2
Create your signature — Choose from three options: draw with mouse/touch, type your name in cursive style, or upload an image of your signature. You can clear and redo as many times as needed.
3
Place and resize — Drag your signature to the exact spot on the page where you want it. Resize by dragging the corners to make it larger or smaller as needed.
4
Sign and download — Click "Sign PDF" button. Your signed document will be ready to download in seconds. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded to any server.

🔒 100% Private & Secure

TronoPDF processes all signature operations directly in your browser. Your PDF document and signature image are never uploaded to any server - they stay on your device. This means your signed contracts, legal documents, and business agreements remain completely private and secure. Electronic signatures created with TronoPDF are accepted for most documents worldwide under laws like ESIGN Act and eIDAS.
