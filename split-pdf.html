How to Split PDF Files

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your document. The tool displays thumbnails of all pages for visual selection.
2
Select pages visually — Click on page thumbnails to select the pages you want to extract. Selected pages are highlighted. You can select individual pages or multiple pages.
3
Choose split option — Select "Extract selected pages" to get one PDF with chosen pages, or "Split by ranges" to create separate PDF files for each range you define.
4
Split and download — Click "Split PDF" button. Your extracted pages or split files download instantly. All processing happens in your browser using pdf-lib - nothing is uploaded to any server.
