How to Convert Excel to PDF

1
Select your Excel file — Click "Select Excel file" button or drag & drop your .xlsx, .xls or .csv file. The tool accepts all common spreadsheet formats.
2
Preview your sheets — See a live table preview of your spreadsheet. Click on sheet tabs to switch between multiple sheets in your workbook before converting.
3
Choose options — Select orientation (Auto, Portrait, Landscape), toggle grid lines and bold header options. Auto mode intelligently chooses the best layout for each sheet.
4
Convert and download — Click "Convert to PDF" button. Your spreadsheet becomes a crisp, professional PDF with every sheet on its own page. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded.
