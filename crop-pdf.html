How to Crop PDF Pages

1
Upload your PDF — Click "Select PDF file" button or drag & drop your document. The tool displays the first page for cropping preview.
2
Choose crop method — Click "Auto-detect" to remove margins automatically, select a preset size (A4, Letter, 16:9), or drag the crop handles to manually select the area you want to keep.
3
Fine-tune if needed — Adjust the crop area by dragging handles or enter exact X, Y, width and height values. Check "Apply to all pages" to crop every page the same way.
4
Apply and download — Click "Apply Crop" button. Your cropped PDF will be ready to download instantly. All processing happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded.
