How to Summarize Text or PDF

1
Add your content — Paste any article, essay or paragraph into the text box, or click "Upload PDF / TXT" to load a document. The algorithm works with text of any reasonable length.
2
Choose summary length — Select Short (10% of original), Medium (20%) or Long (30%) based on how condensed you want the summary to be. Short gives key points only, Long keeps more detail.
3
Click Summarize — Press the Summarize button. The algorithm analyzes your text, scores sentences by importance, and builds a condensed summary in seconds.
4
Copy or download — Review your summary with word reduction stats, then copy it to clipboard or download as a .txt file. Everything happens in your browser - nothing is uploaded.

🔒 Private Processing

Unlike summarizers that send your text to external servers, TronoPDF runs all summarization directly in your browser using an extractive algorithm. Your articles, essays, research papers and business documents never leave your device. This makes it suitable for sensitive content, academic work and confidential business reports - no API key, no signup required.
