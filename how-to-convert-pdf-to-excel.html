What Works Best For PDF To Excel Conversion

✅ Works Great

  • Text-based PDFs with clean tables
  • PDFs generated from Excel, Word or accounting software
  • Bank statements, invoices, financial reports
  • Simple tables with clear borders

⚠️ May Need Adjustments

  • Scanned PDFs (use OCR PDF first)
  • Complex tables with merged cells
  • PDFs with unusual layouts
  • Tables without clear borders

Tips For Best Results

  • Use text-based PDFs — scanned PDFs need OCR first
  • Check column alignment — verify data is in correct columns
  • Review numbers — always verify important financial data
  • Keep the original PDF — as a reference
  • Test on a single page first if the PDF is very large
  • Convert back to PDF after editing using Excel to PDF

⚠️ Always verify converted data, especially financial figures. While conversion is usually accurate, complex tables may need manual cleanup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will formulas work in the converted Excel?

No. Conversion extracts data only, not formulas. You'll need to add formulas yourself in Excel.

Can I convert scanned PDFs?

Use OCR PDF first to make the text selectable, then convert to Excel.

Can I open XLSX in Google Sheets?

Yes. XLSX files open in Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel, LibreOffice Calc, and most spreadsheet apps.

Is there a file size limit?

No server limit since processing happens in your browser. Very large files depend on your device's memory.
